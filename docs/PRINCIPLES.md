# Lyte Enterprise principles

1. Signal over dashboard volume.
2. Business impact beside technical evidence.
3. Declared topology without invented causality.
4. AI-agent quality, cost, privacy, and safety as first-class telemetry.
5. Human-owned decisions and fail-closed effectors.
6. Source identity, truth labels, receipts, and playback on every consequential conclusion.
7. Original, accessible, performance-bounded product design.
