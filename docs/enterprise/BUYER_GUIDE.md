# Buyer guide

Lyte is a governed business-observability command system for organizations
whose revenue, customer experience, regulated operations, or service delivery
depend on complex digital systems.

- Engineering observability explains software and infrastructure behavior.
- BI and warehouses explain historical business data.
- Incident management coordinates response work.
- AIOps ranks or correlates operational signals.
- FinOps explains technology cost.
- AI-agent observability explains model, tool, latency, quality, and safety
  behavior.
- Lyte links those evidence planes to customer journeys, economic outcomes,
  human decisions, verification, and a replayable receipt chain.

Lyte does not require a rip-and-replace adoption. A design partner starts with
one revenue-critical journey, its three to ten services, one delivery source,
relevant agents, and two or three outcomes. Expansion follows measured value.

No benchmark or capability count is used to claim Lyte is better than another
vendor. Comparative statements require a reproducible fixture and result.
