# Lyte Enterprise go-to-market

Lyte lands with one revenue-critical journey and a technical sponsor, then expands into a cross-functional operating layer.

## Initial sponsor

- VP Engineering, SRE, Platform Engineering, or AI Platform.

## Expansion coalition

- CIO or CTO for operating posture;
- COO or business operations for journey outcomes;
- FinOps or CFO for unit economics and revenue exposure;
- risk, compliance, and audit for evidence and playback.

## Pilot shape

- one critical journey;
- three to ten services;
- one delivery source;
- one or more AI agents where applicable;
- two or three measurable outcomes;
- named human owners and evidence references.

Lyte should normally coexist with existing telemetry systems during the pilot. It wins by connecting those signals to customer, cost, revenue, risk, and governed decisions rather than forcing a replacement project on day one.
