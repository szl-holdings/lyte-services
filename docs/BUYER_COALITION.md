# Buyer coalition

Lyte is purchased and adopted by a coalition rather than one isolated persona.

| Stakeholder | Primary value |
|---|---|
| Engineering, SRE, and platform | Service health, SLO burn, topology, delivery, and investigation priority |
| AI platform and agent operations | Agent cost, latency, success, privacy, safety, and outcome linkage |
| CIO and CTO | One evidence-backed operating posture across systems and business outcomes |
| COO and business operations | Customer-journey performance, accountable owners, and cost of delay |
| FinOps and CFO | Unit economics, cost per successful outcome, and revenue exposure |
| Risk, compliance, and audit | Truth labels, source identity, review boundaries, receipts, and playback |
